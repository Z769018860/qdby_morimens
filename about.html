怎么玩

  1. 每道题是一份匿名的融灾战报：区域、回合数、出牌数、单回合最高伤害、伤害构成，以及队内 4 人的伤害、格挡、治疗。
  2. 队员按伤害从高到低标为 A–D 位。你要在对应位置填入唤醒体，以及（取决于难度档）命轮、密契、钥令、潜能进阶和造物。
  3. 提交后：🟩 位置/归属正确；🟨 在队内但放错了位置或装在别人身上；⬛ 不在队内。潜能进阶用 ↑/↓ 提示大小，相差 2 以内显示黄色。
  4. 最多提交 6 次。提示可以帮你缩小范围，但每次消耗 6 积分（积分靠答对获得，见下）。
  5. 即使只猜唤醒体（入门档），结算时也会给出完整的命轮、密契、钥令、造物与唤醒体属性，方便学习完整配队。

难度档与难度分

  • 入门：只猜 4 名唤醒体；进阶：+ 命轮（每人 2 件）；老手：+ 密契套装、钥令；大师：+ 潜能进阶、全部造物。
  • 每道题另有一个 难度分（0–100），只由公开信息决定，三项加权： 信息排除度（把回合数/出牌数/能量/单回合最高伤害/死扛/剩余生命做粗分桶后，题库里同桶的题越多，越难靠排除法锁定）； 伤害集中度（队内伤害越集中在一两人身上，越容易判断主 C，越简单）； 数值常见度（回合数、出牌数是否为常见的整数，越“整”越好记）。
  • 题目按难度分从低到高切成 4 档，所以档位是循序渐进的。

忘却前夜配队规则（给圈外人）

  • 界域（阵营）：每个唤醒体属于四大界域之一——混沌、血肉、深海、超维。
  • 组队：除「混沌」外，唤醒体只能与「自身界域 + 混沌」组队；「混沌」可与任意界域组队。
  • 队伍构成：4 名唤醒体 + 1 名守密人（你，携带钥令技能）。
  • 养成：每个唤醒体有 2 个命轮（武器）、1 套密契（3/6 件套效果）、指令卡（打击/防御/技能）、天赋（疯狂预兆、灵塑适性等）与潜能进阶（灵塑）。
  • 狂气 vs 算力：狂气是大招能量条，集满触发「狂气爆发」——消耗的是狂气，不是算力；算力是每回合打出指令卡所消耗的资源。
  • 狂气等级指「疯狂预兆」天赋的等级（开局获得狂气）；灵塑等级指「灵塑适性」天赋 / 潜能进阶的等级。
  • 造物：探索过程中获得的道具/遗物。
  • 伤害来源：战报里的守密人伤害（你的钥令）、触腕、反击、力量、侵蚀/中毒，能帮你锁定队伍构筑。

账号与个人中心

本站是纯静态站点，没有自己的服务器，账号直接复用 Waline 的现成服务（和 忘忘看报 用的是同一套），数据存在真实的数据库里，不需要你自己部署。

  • 只有两种状态：游客（不登录，进度读本机 localStorage）和账号（登录后进度跟账号走）。没有「本地昵称」这类中间态——想留名字就注册，不想注册就用游客。
  • 注册 / 登录：右上角头像，或「个人中心」页面。用户名 2–20 个字符（中文可以），密码至少 6 位；用户名在后台会映射成 用户名@zjtt.local 的邮箱形式，所以不需要你填邮箱。
  • 个人中心 = 账号数据中枢：昵称与头像、偏好唤醒体、推理记录、工坊战报、典藏进度、积分与失败题库，全都在这一页，也全都会随账号同步。右上角有「上次同步」时间和「立即同步」按钮。
  • 每一局都落库：每日一题、随机一题、自建题、工坊导入的回放，结算后都会写一条记录（时间、题目、模式、档位、胜负、评级、分数、回放链接），同一道题只计一次；最多保留最近 300 条。
  • 偏好唤醒体：在个人中心选好之后，推理页会出现「只看偏好唤醒体」开关，勾上就只从包含这些唤醒体的题目里出题（先按成员哈希分桶粗筛，再逐题解密确认，所以不用每次解密整库）。
  • 头像：内置 70 个预设 —— 62 位唤醒体的官方立绘 + 8 张精选 CG，都是 96×96 的方图；也可以直接贴一个图片直链（≤250 字符，例如 QQ 头像）。头像存在账号里，换设备登录也在。
  • 云端档案怎么存：把「积分 / 解锁 CG / 失败题库 / 统计 / 偏好 / 工坊题 / 推理记录 / 背景主题」打成一个 ZJ1D: + base64 的文档，存进 Waline 评论表里属于你自己的那一条（路径 /zjtt/u/<id>-<散列>，不暴露自增 id）。首次建档用一次 POST，之后一律 PUT 更新，避开评论频率限制。
  • 合并规则：换设备登录时，积分取两边较大值，解锁 CG 与失败题库取并集，统计逐项取大，推理记录与工坊题按 id 去重取新——所以两台设备交替玩也不会互相覆盖。退出登录只清本机的同步凭据，云端档案保留。

图鉴与关键词

图鉴收录全部唤醒体（含技能、天赋、启灵、衍生卡牌与推荐配置）、命轮、密契、钥令、造物与怪物。文字里的状态与机制会显示为带图标的小标签：悬停可看说明，点击可展开详情，并一键跳转到「包含该关键词」的条目列表。顶部的「关键词」页收录了全部 100 个关键词。

中英文对照以官方中文为准：名称与术语优先取自中文维基、官方游戏文本表（Morimens-Localizations）与游戏客户端配置；关键词详情里会标注译名来源，凡未在官方文本中核实的会标为「非官方译名（站内译）」，仅供参考。

积分、提示与解锁

  • 获取：答对一道题按难度给分，公式为 8 + 难度分 × 1.2（难度分 0–100，所以一题大约 8–128 分，答对后四舍五入）。积分存在浏览器本地（zjtt:credits），换设备/清缓存会归零。
  • 花在哪：① 买提示，每次 6 分；② 解锁典藏馆的 CG。
  • 提示：入口在队伍卡的「提示」按钮，分「缩小唤醒体范围」「看命轮/密契」「看钥令/造物」几类，扣分即时生效，买过的提示不会因为你换页而退还。

失败题库

一道题用尽 6 次提交仍未答对，就会标为「失败」并移出出题池——它不会再随机出现，每日一题遇到失败题也会顺延到下一题。失败题库面板保留这些题目的答案（含完整队伍、命轮、密契、钥令、造物与回放），所以失败的题仍然可以点开当「复盘」学习，只是不能再作答。

CG 典藏馆

典藏馆收录了从中文维基整理的全部官方 CG（438 张，另有 7 张在维基上已失效），按「典藏馆 / 主线剧情 / 特遣记录 / 动态绘卷 / 场景 CG / 其他」分组。未解锁的图以模糊缩略图呈现，点击按分组价格扣积分解锁（典藏馆 60 / 动态绘卷 55 / 特遣记录 45 / 主线剧情 40 / 场景 30 / 其他 30）。解锁状态存在本地，终身有效。

大图支持左右翻页（键盘 ← / →）与「查看维基原图」，每张图都保留了灰机 wiki 的原始文件名，方便溯源。顶部还能按标题/文件名搜索（官方 CG 文件名里带章节编号，例如「C09 012」）。

背景主题：解锁任意一张 CG 后，卡片和放大视图里都会出现「设为背景」。点一下，这张图会成为全站背景（模糊 + 渐隐处理），同时按它自己的主色重新生成整套 UI 配色（底色、面板、边框、文字、强调色、金色），全站六个页面一起生效，存在本地，下次打开还在。构建期已经为 438 张图各算好一套配色（scripts/build_themes.py，其中 407 张是深色系、31 张是浅色系），切换是即时的。再点一次「背景使用中」即可恢复默认。

创意工坊

「工坊」让你自己出题：

  • 导入回放（三种方式）：
    • 回放码：把 battleUuid 粘进去点「按回放码载入」，直接给出完整队伍 —— 4 名唤醒体（等级 / 灵塑 / 突破）、每人 2 件命轮、密契、钥令、8 个造物，全部来自题库答案。
    • 回放文件：把官方回放（BattleReplay_*.json）下载下来用「选回放 JSON 文件」读入。文件其实是 LZ4 帧 + MessagePack（不是 JSON），解码器沿用 qdby_chinese 的回放复盘实现，在浏览器本地展开，不会上传。解析出的命轮（每人 2 件，来自战斗里挂载的「状态@武器」）、造物、钥令、等级与灵塑都准确；密契只有在本局触发过才会留下痕迹，所以若这条回放在题库里，会自动回查题库把密契补全，否则需要手选。
    • 粘贴摘要：战报摘要 JSON 直接粘进文本框也行。
    浏览器有跨域限制，不能直接抓回放地址（对象存储没开 CORS），所以先用「打开官方回放」下载，再选文件。
  • 手动出题：4 个位置各选唤醒体 / 2 件命轮 / 密契，再配等级·灵塑、钥令、造物与回合数等元数据。
  • 分享：生成 ZJ2-… 题目代码，好友打开 index.html?p=ZJ2-… 就能直接挑战；也可以选择上传到云题库再用代码取回。
  • 自建题池：保存的题进入「我的题目」，会混入你的随机/每日题目池，答对同样给积分。

回放与复盘

答对（或在失败题库里查看）之后，结果面板会给出该局的官方回放地址：

  • 官方回放：https://z1g-warreport.qookkagames.com/publish/BattleReplay_<uuid>.json，可直接下载原始 JSON。
  • 忘忘看报复盘：一键把 uuid 送到 morimens-tools 回放复盘，看逐回合的伤害、格挡、治疗与手牌曲线。
  • 复制 uuid：方便贴到别处或自己存档。1000 道题全部匹配到了唯一战报（联合键 赛季+档位+分数+回合+出牌+单回合最高），没有歧义。

推理思路示例

  • 伤害占比 85% 以上、单回合最高伤害极高的 A 位，基本是主 C。
  • 格挡很高、几乎没有伤害的位置，多半是防御型；有治疗数字的位置，说明带了治疗手段。
  • 战报的伤害构成（触腕、反击、力量、侵蚀）是强线索：比如触腕伤害高说明队伍有深海/触腕体系，力量高说明有叠力量的角色。
  • 养成线索（指令卡总等级、狂气等级、灵塑）也能缩小范围：主 C 的指令卡通常等级最高。
  • Boss 的阵营、回合数和出牌数，能帮你判断是快攻还是慢热体系。去「图鉴」查各角色、命轮、密契的效果与本期使用率，就是你的推理工具书。

状态/机制图标取自 SKeyDB 的战斗图标库（Battle_Card_Buff_*、界域徽记 realm-badge-*），按官方 iconId 与词条映射，共 30 个图标覆盖 39 个常用关键词；没有官方图标的词条仍用文字字形显示。CG 与唤醒体档案（生日、身高、体重、诺斯指数、获取途径、语音）来自中文维基的角色条目与图片库。回放指向官方战报服务与忘忘看报复盘工具，本站不转存战斗数据。

题库共 2000 道：data/puzzles.json（1000 道核心题，每日一题只从这里出，保证扩充题库异步加载时题目不跳变）与 data/puzzles-extra.json（1000 道扩充题）。难度分统一重算后按分位切成 4 档（1 档 560 / 2 档 560 / 3 档 520 / 4 档 360），分档依据是「同签名题数」分布与伤害构成熵等公开信息，构建脚本 scripts/finalize_puzzles.py 会一并做结构审计：队伍 4 人唯一、命轮 1–2 件、密契齐全、钥令有效、造物 ≤18、UUID 完整、分桶号 4 个、公开线索 4 条；当前全部 2000 道通过，且没有任何两道题的公开线索完全重复。

数据由 qdby_chinese 生成，脚本见 scripts/build_data.py、scripts/build_puzzles_more.py、scripts/enhance_data.py、scripts/build_official_terms.py、scripts/score_difficulty.py、scripts/finalize_puzzles.py。

How to play

  1. Each puzzle is an anonymised Disaster raid report: zone, rounds, cards played, highest single-round damage, damage breakdown, plus each of the 4 members' damage / block / healing.
  2. Members are labelled A–D by damage, highest first. Fill each slot with an Awakener and — depending on the level — Wheels, Covenants, a Keeper skill, Potency and Creations.
  3. After submitting: 🟩 right place/owner; 🟨 in the team but wrong slot or attached to someone else; ⬛ not in the team. Potency uses ↑/↓, and shows yellow within 2.
  4. Six submissions max. Hints narrow things down but cost 6 credits each (credits are earned by solving, see below).
  5. Even at the beginner level (Awakeners only), the result reveals the full Wheels, Covenants, Keeper skill, Creations and Awakener stats, so you learn complete team building.

Levels and difficulty score

  • Beginner: 4 Awakeners. Advanced: + Wheels (2 each). Veteran: + Covenants and Keeper skill. Master: + Potency and all Creations.
  • Every puzzle also has a difficulty score (0–100) derived only from public clues, weighted from three parts: clue exclusion (coarse buckets of rounds / cards / energy / max hit / death-resist / HP left — the more puzzles share the same bucket, the harder it is to narrow down); damage concentration (the more a team's damage is concentrated on one or two members, the easier the carry is to spot); number familiarity (whether rounds and card counts are common round numbers).
  • Puzzles are sorted by that score into four tiers, so the ladder is gradual.

Team-building rules (for newcomers)

  • Realms: every Awakener belongs to one of Chaos, Caro, Aequor or Ultra.
  • Mixing: apart from Chaos, an Awakener can only team with its own realm plus Chaos; Chaos can team with any realm.
  • Team: 4 Awakeners + 1 Keeper (you, carrying a Keeper skill).
  • Progression: 2 Wheels (weapons), one Covenant set (3/6-piece effects), command cards (Strike / Defense / Skill), talents (Madness Omen, Soulforge Aptitude…) and Potency.
  • Aliemus vs Arithmetica: Aliemus is the ultimate gauge — Exalt consumes Aliemus, not Arithmetica; Arithmetica is what you spend to play command cards.
  • Madness level is the level of the Madness Omen talent (grants Aliemus at the start); Potency level is Soulforge Aptitude / Potency progression.
  • Creations: items/relics picked up during a run.
  • Damage sources: Keeper damage (your skill), Tentacle, Counter, Strength and Poison/Erosion in the report are strong clues about the build.

Account and profile

This is a static site with no server of its own, so accounts reuse an existing Waline service (the same one behind qdby_chinese). Users live in a real database — nothing for you to deploy.

  • Two states only: guest (not signed in, progress read from this browser's localStorage) or account (signed in, progress follows the account). There is no in-between “local nickname” — register if you want a name, stay a guest if you don't.
  • Register / sign in: the avatar in the top-right, or the Profile page. Usernames are 2–20 characters (Chinese is fine), passwords at least 6; the username is mapped to name@zjtt.local internally, so no email is required.
  • Profile = the account's data hub: display name and avatar, preferred Awakeners, solve history, workshop reports, gallery progress, credits and the failed bank all live here — and all of it syncs with the account. “Last synced” and a “Sync now” button sit in the header.
  • Every run is stored: daily, random, custom and workshop-imported puzzles each write one record after grading (time, puzzle, mode, level, win/loss, rating, score, replay link). A puzzle only counts once; the latest 300 are kept.
  • Preferred Awakeners: pick them on the Profile page and the puzzle screen grows a “preferred Awakeners only” switch. When on, only puzzles containing those Awakeners are drawn — members are hashed into buckets first, then each candidate is decrypted to confirm, so the whole library is never decrypted per draw.
  • Avatars: 70 presets — the official portrait of all 62 awakeners plus 8 hand-picked CGs, all 96×96 squares; or paste your own image URL (≤250 chars, e.g. a QQ avatar). Avatars live on the account, so they follow you across devices.
  • How the cloud document works: credits / unlocked CG / failed bank / stats / preferences / workshop puzzles / solve history / backdrop theme are packed into a ZJ1D: + base64 document stored on your own row in Waline's comment table (path /zjtt/u/<id>-<hash>, no auto-increment id exposed). One POST creates it, every later write is a PUT, which sidesteps the comment rate limit.
  • Merge rules: when you sign in elsewhere, credits take the larger value, unlocked CG and the failed bank are unioned, stats take the per-key maximum, and history / workshop puzzles are de-duplicated by id keeping the newest — so alternating between two devices never clobbers the other. Signing out only clears local sync credentials; the cloud document stays.

Codex and keywords

The codex lists every Awakener (with skills, talents, enlightenments, derived cards and recommended builds), Wheel, Covenant, Keeper skill, Creation and monster. States and mechanics in the text appear as icon chips: hover for a summary, click for details and a jump to every entry that uses them. The Keywords tab covers all 100 terms.

Chinese names follow official Chinese text wherever it exists — the Chinese wiki, the official game text sheet (Morimens-Localizations) and the game client config. Keyword details show the provenance of each translation; anything not confirmed in official text is marked “Unofficial (site rendering)” and is for reference only.

Credits, hints and unlocks

  • Earning: solving a puzzle pays out by difficulty, 8 + difficulty score × 1.2 (difficulty 0–100, so roughly 8–128 credits, rounded). Credits live in local storage (zjtt:credits) — clearing site data or switching devices resets them.
  • Spending: (1) hints at 6 credits each; (2) unlocking gallery CGs.
  • Hints sit on the team card. They are charged the moment you use them and are not refunded on page changes.

Failed bank

Burn all six submissions without solving and the puzzle is marked failed and removed from the draw pool — it will not come up randomly again, and the daily puzzle skips past failed ones. The failed bank panel keeps those answers (full team, Wheels, Covenants, Keeper skill, Creations and replay), so a failure still works as a review — you just cannot answer it again.

CG gallery

The gallery holds every official CG collected from the Chinese wiki — 438 images (7 more are dead links on the wiki), grouped into Hall / Main story / Dispatch records / Dynamic scrolls / Scene CGs / Other. Locked images show as a blurred thumbnail; clicking spends credits to unlock (Hall 60 / Dynamic 55 / Dispatch 45 / Story 40 / Scene 30 / Other 30). Unlocks are stored locally and are permanent.

The viewer supports arrow-key paging and a “view original on the wiki” link — every item keeps its original wiki filename for provenance. A search box filters by title or filename (official CG filenames carry the chapter number, e.g. “C09 012”).

Backdrop themes: once a CG is unlocked, both the card and the viewer offer “Use as backdrop”. That CG becomes the site background (blurred and faded) and its own dominant colours are turned into a full UI palette — surface, panel, borders, text, accent and gold — across all six pages, remembered locally. All 438 palettes are precomputed at build time (scripts/build_themes.py; 407 dark, 31 light), so switching is instant. Click “In use” again to go back to the default look.

Workshop

The workshop lets you author your own puzzles:

  • Import a replay (three ways):
    • Replay code: paste the battleUuid and hit “Load by replay code” — you get the complete team: four awakeners (level / potency / breakthrough), two Wheels each, Covenants, the Keeper skill and all eight Creations, straight from the puzzle-bank answer.
    • Replay file: download the official replay (BattleReplay_*.json) and open it with “Pick replay JSON”. The file is actually LZ4 frame + MessagePack, not JSON — the decoder is the one from qdby_chinese's replay review, running locally; nothing is uploaded. Wheels (two per awakener, read from the mounted “状态@武器” states), Creations, the Keeper skill, level and potency all come out exact; Covenants only leave a trace when they triggered, so if the replay is in the bank the Covenants are filled in from the answer, otherwise pick them by hand.
    • Paste a summary: report-summary JSON in the text box works too.
    Browsers cannot fetch the replay URL directly (the object store sends no CORS headers), so use “Open official replay” to download it first.
  • Manual authoring: 4 slots × (Awakener, 2 Wheels, Covenant), plus level/potency, Keeper skill, Creations and rounds.
  • Sharing: produces a ZJ2-… code — friends open index.html?p=ZJ2-… and play immediately. Uploading to the cloud bank and loading it back by code is also supported.
  • Your pool: saved puzzles go under “My puzzles” and join your random/daily pool, paying credits like any other.

Replays and review

After a solve (or from the failed bank) the result panel offers the official replay for that run:

  • Official replay: https://z1g-warreport.qookkagames.com/publish/BattleReplay_<uuid>.json — the raw JSON.
  • Wangwang review: sends the uuid to the morimens-tools replay review for per-round damage, block, healing and hand curves.
  • Copy uuid: for posting or archiving. All 1000 puzzles match exactly one report (joint key: season + rank + score + rounds + cards + max hit) with zero ambiguity.

Example reasoning

  • An A slot with 85%+ of team damage and a huge max hit is almost certainly the carry.
  • A slot with high block and almost no damage is likely a defensive Awakener; healing numbers mean a healing source.
  • The damage breakdown is a strong clue: high Tentacle damage implies an Aequor/tentacle build, high Strength implies a STR-stacking unit.
  • Progression clues (total command-card level, madness level, potency) narrow it down too — the carry usually has the highest card levels.
  • Boss realm, rounds and card counts hint at fast or slow builds. Use the codex for effects and pick rates — it is your detective's handbook.

Data notes

Reports come from the public Disaster leaderboards (seasons 71 and 72); player UIDs are removed. Support-type slots are hard to pin down uniquely, so scoring is per-field accuracy rather than all-or-nothing. Answers are obfuscated, but a pure static site cannot truly prevent cheating.

Skill data from SKeyDB (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, non-commercial). Official Chinese from Morimens-Localizations and the Chinese wiki.

Data generated from qdby_chinese; see scripts/build_data.py, scripts/build_puzzles_more.py, scripts/enhance_data.py, scripts/build_official_terms.py, scripts/score_difficulty.py and scripts/finalize_puzzles.py.

Status/mechanic icons come from SKeyDB's battle icon set (Battle_Card_Buff_*, realm badges realm-badge-*), mapped by official iconId: 30 icons cover 39 common keywords; terms without official art fall back to a text glyph. CGs and Awakener profiles (birthday, height, weight, Nos index, acquisition, voice lines) come from the Chinese wiki. Replays point at the official report service and the Wangwang review tool — no battle data is mirrored here.

All progress (credits, unlocked CGs, failed bank, authored puzzles, preferred Awakeners, answer history, language, backdrop theme) is kept in localStorage under the zjtt: prefix by default; once signed in it is packed into an account document and synced to the cloud, so another device restores it on sign-in, and signing out returns to local-only. Puzzles uploaded to the cloud bank are public — do not put anything private there.

The puzzle set holds 2000 entries: data/puzzles.json (1000 core puzzles — the daily puzzle is drawn only from these, so it never jumps while the extra file loads asynchronously) and data/puzzles-extra.json (1000 extra). Difficulty is recomputed across both and split into four tiers by percentile (tier 1: 560 / 2: 560 / 3: 520 / 4: 360), based on public-only signals such as how many puzzles share the same clue signature and the entropy of the damage breakdown. scripts/finalize_puzzles.py also audits every entry (4 unique members, 1–2 Wheels each, Covenants present, valid Keeper skill, ≤18 Creations, intact UUID, 4 bucket ids, 4 public clues); all 2000 pass, and no two puzzles share an identical public clue set.
